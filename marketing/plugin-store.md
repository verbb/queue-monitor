Queue Monitor watches Craft’s background jobs and tells the right people when processing has stalled or failed. Catch a broken queue before an editor or customer has to discover the consequences.

Monitor Craft’s queue for jobs that have failed and send a notification with a direct route back to the relevant control-panel screen. Teams can respond to interrupted imports, emails, transforms, and other background work without checking the queue manually.

## Features

- **Queue awareness:** Watch the background jobs Craft and installed plugins create.
- **Failure detection:** Identify failed work that would otherwise sit unnoticed.
- **Automatic retries:** Retry failed jobs according to the project's configured limits.
- **Email alerts:** Notify the people responsible for keeping the site healthy.
- **Webhook alerts:** Send queue failures and stalled-queue warnings to another monitoring service.
- **Direct follow-up:** Point recipients back to the queue information in Craft.
- **Focused notifications:** Configure monitoring so alerts reflect the project’s operational needs.
- **Native companion:** Add awareness while leaving queue management with Craft.
- **Useful alerts:** Configure who should be notified and the conditions that deserve attention so routine processing does not become inbox noise. Queue Monitor complements Craft’s own queue tools rather than replacing them.
